= Statements and procurement
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

These rules set how a deployment tells people how accessible it is, and how a buyer requires accessibility.
A statement and a contract both rest on the test record of ACC-045.

== Accessibility statements

[[ACC-050]]
*ACC-050.* Each deployment MUST publish an accessibility statement in English and in French.
Every web page MUST link to it, such as from the footer.
A mobile app MUST link to it from its home screen or its settings.
The page from which a person downloads a document MUST link to it as well.
People need to find out what works, what does not, and whom to ask, from wherever they are.

[[ACC-051]]
*ACC-051.* An accessibility statement MUST hold these items, in plain language.

. The standards and level that the deployment targets, starting with ACC-001 and ACC-003, and any standard that its jurisdiction requires.

. The status: full, partial or no conformance.

. The date of the statement and the date of the test record it relies on.

. Each known gap, with a way around it and a planned date to fix it.

. The name and version of each assistive technology used in the tests.

. The meaning of each icon that has no visible text label.

. At least two ways to reach the team, such as an email address and a phone number.

. An offer of help or of another format.

These items meet the statement rules of Canada and Québec in one page.

[[ACC-052]]
*ACC-052.* A deployment MUST NOT claim full conformance while it knows of a failure.
A claim of conformance MUST hold the parts that section 5.3.1 of link:https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/[WCAG 2.2] requires.
These parts are the date, the version, the level, the pages covered and the technologies relied on.
A false claim misleads the people who depend on the product, and the buyer who relies on it.

[[ACC-053]]
*ACC-053.* A deployment MUST update its statement at least every 12 months.
It MUST also update it after each release that changes a known gap.
An old statement soon stops matching the product.

[[ACC-054]]
*ACC-054.* Each release of an {abbr-fpsot-first} product MUST ship a conformance report.
The report MUST give the result of each criterion of ACC-001 and ACC-003.
It MUST also give the result of each clause of link:https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/301500_301599/301549/04.01.01_60/en_301549v040101p.pdf[EN 301 549 V4.1.1] that applies.
Each deployment can then build its statement from the report and its own tests.

== Procurement

[[ACC-055]]
*ACC-055.* A buyer that adopts this specification MUST require link:https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/301500_301599/301549/04.01.01_60/en_301549v040101p.pdf[EN 301 549 V4.1.1] in each call for tenders.
It MUST also require ACC-001 to ACC-003.
When a law names another edition, like link:https://accessible.canada.ca/creating-accessibility-standards/summary-can-asc-en-301-5492024-accessibility-requirements-ict-products-and-services-en-301-5492021-idt[CAN/ASC - EN 301 549:2024], the buyer MUST add it.
EN 301 549 covers hardware, software, documents and support services, and many governments buy against it.
Version 4.1.1 brings it in line with {abbr-wcag-first} 2.2.

[[ACC-056]]
*ACC-056.* A call for tenders MUST ask each bidder for a conformance report with a gap analysis.
The report covers each required standard.
The report MUST rest on a test record that meets ACC-045.
A buyer MUST NOT accept a statement of conformance with no test record behind it.
Evidence lets the buyer compare bids on facts.

[[ACC-057]]
*ACC-057.* Before it awards the contract, a buyer SHOULD test a sample of the leading bid itself.
The test SHOULD use the checks of ACC-043.
The buyer SHOULD also give accessibility a weight in the scoring of bids.
A short test before the award costs less than a fix after it.

[[ACC-058]]
*ACC-058.* A contract MUST require the supplier to fix accessibility failures within an agreed time.
It MUST require a new conformance report with each major release.
Conformance can fall with each update, unless the contract holds the supplier to it.

[[ACC-059]]
*ACC-059.* A project MUST check each outside component against the baseline before it adopts the component.
Outside components include libraries, widgets and themes.
The project MUST record each gap that it finds, and fix it or report it upstream.
A product is only as accessible as its weakest component.
