= FPSOT accessibility specification
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This specification sets how {abbr-fpsot-first} products serve people with disabilities.
It covers web pages, documents and mobile apps, for the public and for staff.
Its baseline is level AA of link:https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/[version 2.2] of the {abbr-wcag-first}, with a few more criteria.
It also sets how teams test a product, how they state its conformance, and how buyers require accessibility.

The rules come in six parts.

* xref:baseline.adoc[Baseline] sets the target and its scope.
* xref:values.adoc[Accessibility values] sets the values for contrast, focus, targets, motion and text alternatives.
* xref:interaction.adoc[Web pages and apps] covers the keyboard, the structure, forms and mobile apps.
* xref:documents-media.adoc[Documents and media] covers tagged documents, audio and video.
* xref:testing.adoc[Testing] covers automated, manual and assistive technology checks, and the test record.
* xref:conformance.adoc[Statements and procurement] covers accessibility statements and buying accessible products.

This specification owns every accessibility rule of {abbr-fpsot}.
The style and diagrams specifications point to its values and never change them.
The writing specification sets plain language, and the internationalization specification marks the language of text.

== Conformance

[quote, 'link:https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc8174[RFC 8174]']
____
The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" in this document are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14 (link:https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc2119[RFC 2119], link:https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc8174[RFC 8174]) when, and only when, they appear in all capitals, as shown here.
____

The keywords follow requirement WRT-010 of the {abbr-fpsot} writing specification.
The French version uses the French keywords of WRT-011.

A product conforms to this specification when it meets every MUST rule.
A deployment conforms when it also meets the rules that name a deployment, such as ACC-050.
A buyer conforms when its calls for tenders meet ACC-055 to ACC-058.
