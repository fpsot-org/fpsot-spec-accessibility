= Baseline

These rules set the accessibility target of every {abbr-fpsot-first} product and what it covers.
The target is one profile, so a single test result serves Canada, Québec and other buyers.

== Target

[[ACC-001]]
*ACC-001.* A product MUST meet every level A and level AA success criterion of link:https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/[version 2.2] of the {abbr-wcag-first}.
It MUST also meet the five conformance requirements of {abbr-wcag} 2.2, such as full pages and complete processes.
Level AA is the level that most laws and standards name.
Version 2.2 is the latest {abbr-w3c-first} Recommendation, and it adds criteria for focus, targets and sign-in.

[[ACC-002]]
*ACC-002.* The baseline MUST apply to every web page, digital document and mobile app that a product publishes or generates.
It applies to screens for staff and administrators as much as to screens for the public.
Public servants with disabilities use the same tools as everyone else.
One scope also keeps one test plan for the whole product.

[[ACC-003]]
*ACC-003.* A product MUST also meet four level AAA criteria of {abbr-wcag} 2.2.
They are 2.3.2 Three Flashes, 2.4.13 Focus Appearance, 2.5.6 Concurrent Input Mechanisms and 3.1.4 Abbreviations.
The Québec standard link:https://www.quebec.ca/gouvernement/ministeres-organismes/cybersecurite-numerique/publications/standard-accessibilite-sites-web[SGQRI 008 3.0] requires these four, so a product that meets them can serve Québec public bodies.
They cost little to meet when a team plans for them from the start.

[[ACC-004]]
*ACC-004.* A deployment in Québec SHOULD offer Québec Sign Language for prerecorded audio that carries key information.
A deployment elsewhere in Canada MAY offer American Sign Language or Québec Sign Language in the same way.
Sign language is the first language of many Deaf people, and captions do not replace it for them.
Success criterion 1.2.6 of {abbr-wcag} 2.2 describes this service.

== Versions and exceptions

[[ACC-005]]
*ACC-005.* A product MUST NOT target an older {abbr-wcag} version, even where a jurisdiction still accepts one.
A product MAY meet a later {abbr-w3c} Recommendation as well.
Drafts of {abbr-wcag} 3 are not a target.
The {abbr-w3c} states that content that meets {abbr-wcag} 2.2 also meets 2.1 and 2.0 at the same level.
For 2.0 and 2.1, it treats criterion 4.1.1 Parsing as met by markup content.
One current target then covers every older rule.

[[ACC-006]]
*ACC-006.* A product MUST NOT exempt any content that its team controls.
Content from a third party, such as an embedded map, MUST appear in the accessibility statement as a known gap.
The deployment MUST offer that content in an accessible form, or give assistance, when a person asks for it.
An exemption for own content would leave a gap that nobody plans to close.
People still need a way to reach the information.
