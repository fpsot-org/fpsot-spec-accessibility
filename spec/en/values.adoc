= Accessibility values
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

These rules fix the values that designers and authors apply to every screen, document and diagram.
The {abbr-fpsot-first} style and diagrams specifications point to this page.
They never restate or change a value.
Each value meets a success criterion of link:https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/[version 2.2] of the {abbr-wcag-first}, and each rule names that criterion.

[cols="2,3,1",options="header"]
|===
|Value |Minimum |Rule
|Text contrast |4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text |ACC-010
|Contrast of controls, states and graphics |3:1 |ACC-011
|Focus indicator |2 {abbr-css-first} pixels thick around the component, with a 3:1 change |ACC-013
|Target size |24 by 24 {abbr-css} pixels, or 44 by 44 on touch screens |ACC-015
|Flashes |3 or fewer in any second |ACC-016
|Moving content |a pause control after 5 seconds, and no motion when reduced motion is on |ACC-017
|Text size and zoom |200% text, and a width of 320 {abbr-css} pixels |ACC-019
|===

== Color and contrast

[[ACC-010]]
*ACC-010.* Text and images of text MUST have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 with their background.
Large text MUST have a ratio of at least 3:1.
Large text is at least 24 {abbr-css} pixels, or at least 18.66 {abbr-css} pixels in bold.
These ratios MUST hold in every theme and mode, and in every state, such as hover or selected.
Low contrast is the most common barrier for people with low vision.
Criterion 1.4.3 sets these ratios.

[[ACC-011]]
*ACC-011.* Each visible part of a control MUST have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1.
The ratio applies against each adjacent color.
It covers the border of a field, a state such as checked, and an icon that carries meaning.
It also covers the lines and areas of each chart and diagram.
People need to see where a control is and what state it is in.
Criterion 1.4.11 sets this ratio.

[[ACC-012]]
*ACC-012.* Color MUST NOT be the only way to show information, a state or an action.
Add text, a shape, a pattern or a position too.
Each chart and diagram MUST label its series or use patterns, and not rely on color alone.
Some people see few colors, and some screens and printers drop color.
Criterion 1.4.1 sets this rule.

== Focus

[[ACC-013]]
*ACC-013.* Each component that takes keyboard focus MUST show a focus indicator that meets criterion 2.4.13.
The indicator MUST be at least as large as a line that is 2 {abbr-css} pixels thick around the component.
The focused and unfocused states of those pixels MUST differ by a contrast ratio of at least 3:1.
A product MUST NOT remove the focus outline of the browser, unless its replacement meets this rule.
People who use a keyboard need to see where they are at every step.

[[ACC-014]]
*ACC-014.* Content that the product adds, such as a sticky header, MUST NOT cover a component with focus completely.
It SHOULD NOT cover the component in part either.
Scroll padding or a layout without fixed overlays meets both.
A covered focus leaves a keyboard user lost on the page.
Criteria 2.4.11 and 2.4.12 set these rules.

== Targets

[[ACC-015]]
*ACC-015.* Each pointer target MUST be at least 24 by 24 {abbr-css} pixels, or meet an exception of criterion 2.5.8.
On a touch screen and in a mobile app, a control SHOULD be at least 44 by 44 {abbr-css} pixels.
Small targets are hard to hit for people with tremors or limited dexterity.
Fingers need larger targets than a mouse pointer, and criterion 2.5.5 sets the larger size.

== Motion

[[ACC-016]]
*ACC-016.* Content MUST NOT flash more than three times in any one-second period.
This rule has no exception for small or dim flashes, as in criterion 2.3.2.
Flashes can cause seizures, and the safe area of a flash depends on screen size and distance.

[[ACC-017]]
*ACC-017.* Content that moves, blinks or scrolls by itself for over five seconds MUST have a pause control.
The control MUST let the person pause, stop or hide the content, as in criterion 2.2.2.
When the person turns on the reduced motion setting, the product MUST stop all motion that is not essential.
In a web page, the {abbr-css} media feature `prefers-reduced-motion` carries this setting.
This meets criterion 2.3.3.
Motion distracts some people and makes others dizzy or sick.

== Text alternatives

[[ACC-018]]
*ACC-018.* Each image, icon, chart and diagram that carries meaning MUST have a text alternative with the same purpose.
A chart or a diagram MUST also have a longer description, such as a data table, next to it.
An image that only decorates MUST have an empty text alternative, so that screen readers skip it.
People who cannot see an image get the same information from the text.
Criterion 1.1.1 sets this rule.
The {abbr-fpsot} documentation and diagrams specifications set where authors write these texts.

== Text size and layout

[[ACC-019]]
*ACC-019.* Content MUST stay usable with text at 200% of its size, as in criterion 1.4.4.
It MUST stay usable at a width of 320 {abbr-css} pixels, as in criterion 1.4.10.
It MUST also stay usable with the text spacing values of criterion 1.4.12.
A product MUST use real text instead of images of text, except for logos, as in criterion 1.4.5.
People with low vision enlarge text, and scrolling in two directions makes a zoomed page hard to read.
