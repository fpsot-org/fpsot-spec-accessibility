= Documents and media
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

These rules cover the documents that a product publishes or generates, and its audio and video.
Documents include notices, letters, reports and forms, and the published specifications.
Each rule names the success criteria of link:https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/[version 2.2] of the {abbr-wcag-first}.

== Documents

[[ACC-030]]
*ACC-030.* A digital document MUST meet the baseline as link:https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag2ict-22/[WCAG2ICT] applies it to documents.
It MUST also meet clause 10 on documents of link:https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/301500_301599/301549/04.01.01_60/en_301549v040101p.pdf[EN 301 549 V4.1.1].
A document is often the only record that a person gets, so it needs the care of a web page.

[[ACC-031]]
*ACC-031.* A product that generates a {abbr-pdf-first} file MUST generate it as tagged {abbr-pdf} from structured source.
The {abbr-fpsot-first} documentation specification sets the {abbr-pdf} formats in its rule DOC-042.
A product MUST NOT add tags to a finished file as its only way to meet this rule.
A product MUST NOT publish a scanned image as the only form of a document.
Tags give screen readers the headings, the reading order and the tables.
Tags added after the fact often get the reading order wrong.

[[ACC-032]]
*ACC-032.* Each document MUST have a title in its metadata, a declared language and headings in order.
Its reading order MUST match its meaning.
Each table MUST mark its header cells.
Each image MUST have a text alternative under ACC-018.
A document longer than 20 pages SHOULD have bookmarks that follow its headings.
Assistive technologies use this structure to read and move through a document.

[[ACC-033]]
*ACC-033.* A service SHOULD publish its content as a web page, and offer a document only as an extra format.
A service MUST NOT require a fillable {abbr-pdf} or another document form when a web form can do the task.
Web pages adapt to zoom, screen size and assistive technologies better than fixed pages.
Fillable document forms often fail with screen readers and on phones.

== Audio and video

[[ACC-034]]
*ACC-034.* Prerecorded video with sound MUST have captions.
It MUST also have audio description when the picture carries information that the sound does not.
Prerecorded audio alone MUST have a transcript, and live video with sound MUST have captions.
Captions MUST follow the language of the sound, and SHOULD exist in each official language of the video.
Deaf, hard of hearing and blind people need another way to get the same information.
Criteria 1.2.1, 1.2.2, 1.2.4 and 1.2.5 set these rules.

[[ACC-035]]
*ACC-035.* A media player MUST work with a keyboard and with assistive technologies.
It MUST offer controls for captions and audio description.
Media MUST NOT play sound by itself for over three seconds without a control to stop it.
Sound that starts by itself covers the voice of a screen reader.
Criterion 1.4.2 sets the rule on sound.
