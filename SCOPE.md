# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT accessibility specification.

The specification covers the accessibility of FPSOT products: web pages, digital documents, audio and video, and mobile apps, for the public and for staff. It sets the conformance target, the values for contrast, focus, target size, motion and text alternatives, how teams test a product and record the results, the accessibility statement of each deployment, and how buyers require accessibility in procurement.

It does not cover the visual design of themes, nor how diagrams are drawn, which other FPSOT specifications set on top of its values. It is not legal advice.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
